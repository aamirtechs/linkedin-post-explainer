LinkedIn Post Explainer - v1.0.0

Explain, don't generate.

Paste a LinkedIn post and get the part no comment generator gives you: what the post is actually arguing, what evidence it stands on, how much of it is slop or bait, and whether a reply is worth your time at all.

Safe by design

Paste-mode only. This tool never connects to LinkedIn, never reads your feed, never scrapes and never posts anything. You paste a post, everything is analysed locally in your browser, and any draft stays a draft until you decide what to do with it. That is deliberate: LinkedIn's rules ban extensions that scrape or automate the site, so this tool does none of that.
